# TRUSTORA – Privacy Policy **Last Updated: 6 November 2026** ## 1. Introduction Welcome to TRUSTORA. We respect your privacy and aim to protect the information you share through our website. ## 2. Information We Collect When you create a surprise, you may provide: - Recipient's name - Personal messages - Photos and videos - Other information you choose to submit ## 3. How We Use Information We use submitted information to: - Create personalized surprise links. - Display messages and uploaded media to recipients. - Operate and improve TRUSTORA. ## 4. Media Storage Photos and videos are stored using our cloud storage provider, Supabase. Media links are generated temporarily when a surprise is opened. Do not upload private, sensitive, or confidential material. ## 5. Sharing Anyone who has access to a surprise link may be able to view its content. Please share links carefully and only upload content you have permission to share. ## 6. Data Security We use reasonable technical measures to protect information. However, no online service can guarantee complete security. ## 7. Children's Privacy TRUSTORA is not intended to collect personal information from children without appropriate parent or guardian involvement. ## 8. Changes We may update this Privacy Policy when our service changes. Updates will be posted on this page. ## 9. Contact For privacy questions or requests, please contact the TRUSTORA website operator through the contact details provided on our website.
